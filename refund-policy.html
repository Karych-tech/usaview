Refund Policy

How cancellations and refunds work for USAView subscriptions.

Explore Our Plans Contact Support

Last Updated: January 2026

The information below explains our refund process.

1. 24-Hour Refund Window

If you are not satisfied with the Service, you may request a full refund within 24 hours of your initial purchase, as long as the Service has not been heavily used.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact our support team by email or through the Contact page and include the email address used for your purchase. We will review your request and respond as soon as possible.

3. When Refunds Are Processed

Approved refunds are typically processed within 3–5 business days. The time it takes for the refund to appear on your statement may vary depending on your payment provider.

4. Cancellations

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation stops future billing; refunds for the current billing period follow the policy described above.

5. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds may not be available where the Service was used extensively, where a refund request is made after the 24-hour window, or where abuse of the Service is detected.

6. Contact

Questions about this Refund Policy? Reach out at usaview.us@gmail.com and our team will help.
