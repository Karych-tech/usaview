📡 IPTV Explained

What Is IPTV and How Does It Work?

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) streams live TV channels, movies and series over your internet connection — no satellite dish or cable box required.

Streaming Over the Internet

Instead of receiving broadcast signals, IPTV delivers content as data packets over the internet. With your USAView subscription you receive an M3U playlist URL or Xtream Codes login — a server address, username and password.

You enter these details into a supported streaming app such as IPTV Smarters Pro or IBO Player Pro, and the app organizes your live channels, movies, series and EPG program guide into an easy-to-browse interface.
